# Pilot Evaluation Plan

## Evidence integrity

- 100% of public sentences map to approved claim IDs.
- 0 unsupported measured-outcome claims.
- 100% of high-risk cases follow mandatory review route.
- 100% of primary sources have snapshot/hash/date before evidence locking.

## Extraction quality

- Critical metadata precision target: at least 95%.
- Status classification precision target: at least 95%.
- Claim-class accuracy reported separately.
- False-negative review routing must be zero in the pilot gold set.

## Efficiency

Track human research time per case, human review time per case, percentage of
fields accepted without correction, and number of stale/broken sources detected
automatically.

## Public usefulness

Evaluate whether users can find a hazard-, organization-, or goal-relevant case,
identify the case stage, name one transferability limitation, and avoid treating
the card as a copy-paste recipe.

## Accessibility and maintenance

Check keyboard navigation, reduced motion, alt text/credit completeness, source
freshness visibility, and API/static fallback behavior.
